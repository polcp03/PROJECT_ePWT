## Open points found while building the lessons (4-Nov)

- **VESC limits disagree:** `03_hardware/vesc/MOTOR_1.9.xml` (10-Aug) has motor 119 A / battery
  99 A; `motor_params.m` and the baseline say 120 A / 40 A. Read the live values in VESC Tool.
- **VESC current gains:** the XML has kp 0.102 / ki 18.83 (1000 rad/s); `motor_params.m` has
  kp 0.2119 / ki 56.20 (2000 rad/s). Lessons use `motor_params.m`; confirm on the device.
- **Control period still open** (15 or 30 kHz). The `piloop` lab samples at 30 kHz.
- `motor_params.m` comments give τ = 3.82 / 3.49 ms; the right values are 4.02 / 3.54 ms
  (used as a find-the-error in C03-SA, not fixed yet).
