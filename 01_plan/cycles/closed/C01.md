## Why this goal and not the one in the roadmap

The roadmap had C01 as "characterise the plant: R, Ke, poles, vehicle". **Half of it is already
done.** In C00, `p`, `λm`, `R`, `Ld/Lq`, `J` and the rotor drag were measured, and three
independent methods agree on `λm` within 0.02 %. The machine is today the best-characterised
part of the quad.

The vehicle is the worst. `vehicle_params.m` says it plainly: the rolling radius is an
assumption, the mass an estimate, and `Crr`, `CdA`, `eta_drive` and `R_int` are `NaN`. Its own
sensitivity block orders the work:

| Parameter | Status | Error in the predicted acceleration |
|---|---|---|
| `r_roll` | ASSUMED | ±6 % |
| `m_total` | ESTIMATED | ±5 % |
| `Crr` | MISSING | unknown |
| `eta_drive` | MISSING | unknown |
| `CdA` | MISSING | weak below 50 km/h |
| `J_rotor` | MEASURED ±14 % | **±0.06 %** |

One more hour on the motor buys less than a minute on the chassis. C01 goes where the error is.

> [!tip] And there is a meeting reason, not only an engineering one
> What hooked the mentors in C00 **was the method, not the measurements**. The second time,
> the method no longer impresses. On 12 October the method has to be shown **producing a
> number that did not exist before**.

### D3 — Geometry and masses `[bench]`
- [ ] **Roll-out for `r_roll`**: 10 wheel turns with the rider seated, measure the distance,

- [ ] **Static weight distribution**, with a scale under each axle

> [!success] What the weight distribution buys
> It removes the `ASSUMED` from the gear ratio slide. Today the argument "I am at the grip
> limit" rests on an invented μ = 0.75 and 65 % rear. With the distribution measured, half the
> assumption goes — and the other half (μ) can be bounded the day a real maximum acceleration
> is measured.

### D4 — The test that decides C02 `[bench]`

## Out of scope, on purpose

**Simulink.** The longitudinal model is C02. Building it now would mean feeding it the `NaN`s
this cycle exists to fill, and a model parameterised with assumptions cannot be correlated
against anything — it can only be tuned until it looks nice, which is the most expensive way of
not learning.

What would change this: D4 going wrong **and** the whole coast-down campaign falling through.
Then there are days to spare and it does make sense to bring level 1 of the model forward with
the parameters available, labelled.

**Firmware, VCU, data logger.** C05, unless D4 shows the USB doesn't hold. See the
[roadmap](../../roadmap.md).
