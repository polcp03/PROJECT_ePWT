2026-11-06 — Axle loads and CG height: results

Raw readings in readings.md. Wheelbase L = 0.72 m, track 0.46 m front / 0.44 m rear.

01

  • The scales over-read at high load: 120.7 kg against 113.9 kg known. Fractions are trusted, absolute kg are not. Vehicle mass stays at the 38 kg of the 2026-10-08 single-scale reading.
  • CG along the wheelbase: empty 36 cm ahead of the rear axle; with rider 24 cm. The rider sits ≈ 18 cm ahead of the rear axle.
  • Front axle left/right: 11.5 / 8.4 kg empty, but the rear goes the other way (9.5 / 10.0). Not a lateral CG offset: most likely a diagonal load from the larger front-left tyre on a rigid four-wheel chassis (FL + RR carry more), or a scale bias. Swapping the scales would separate them.
02

CG height, empty (tilt test)

Moments about the contact of the lifted axle, with h′ the CG height above the axle-centre line:

N_lower = W · [ a_lower/L + (h′/L) · tanθ ]      sinθ = Δh / L

a_lower is the horizontal distance from the CG to the raised axle on level ground, i.e. the lower axle's share of the wheelbase. N_lower rises linearly with tanθ; the slope is Wh′/L. Δh = block − 3 cm scale = 6.5 and 13 cm.

  • h_cg empty = h′ + loaded wheel radius (≈ 0.16 m, ASSUMED) ≈ 0.31 m, ± 0.08 m.
  • The two series disagree by a factor of two: the signal is only 1–2.5 kg against ±0.5 kg per scale. The number is good to about ±25 %.
  • 3 cm scale correction: W·h′·tan(3/72)/L ≈ 0.3 kg. Below the scale noise, so ignored.
03

CG height with rider (estimated, not measured)

  • The rider's CG is not at the seat (0.45 m). Seated, knees at 90°: trunk, head and arms (~2/3 of body mass) sit well above the seat, legs below it → body CG ≈ 0.15–0.20 m above the seat → ≈ 0.62 m.
  • Combined: (38 × 0.31 + 74.5 × 0.62) / 112.5 ≈ 0.51 m (range 0.45–0.55 m). Putting the rider at the seat would give 0.40 m: 20 % too low.
  • h_cg / L ≈ 0.7. A tall vehicle for its wheelbase: the rider dominates.
04

What it means (with rider, h = 0.51 m)

EffectFormulaNumber
Load transfer under accelerationΔN = m·a·h/L≈ 8 kg to the rear per 0.1 g
Front wheels lift (wheelie)a/g = b/h, b = CG to rear axle0.24 / 0.51 ≈ 0.47 g
Rear wheelspin limit (rear drive)a/g = μ·(l_f/L) / (1 − μ·h/L)μ = 0.4 → 0.37 g; μ = 0.6 → 0.70 g
Static rollover (rigid, no compliance)a_y/g = t/(2h)0.44 / 1.02 ≈ 0.43 g
  • Dirt (μ ≈ 0.4): the rears spin before the front lifts. On high grip the front would lift first, if the drivetrain can push ≈ 520 N at the wheels (not checked).
  • Rollover ≈ 0.43 g is an upper bound: tyre and frame compliance lower it. On dirt the quad slides before it tips (μ < 0.43); on asphalt it can tip first. That is why you lean in.
  • Traction control on the VCU needs h and the split: both now have numbers.
05

If it is ever repeated

Rider on board for the tilt; lift 25–30 cm instead of 13 (a bigger signal against the same noise); swap the scales left/right once; one scale per wheel, all four at the same time.
